Coins is the gift price paid by the viewer. Diamonds are a separate creator reward unit — GiftStream never converts diamonds into coin prices. A price is marked as source-verified only when TikTok or LIVE explicitly reports a coin cost; when that field is missing, GiftStream simply shows “price unavailable” instead of an error or a spinner. A viewer price is never derived from creator diamonds. Prices may differ by region and can change on TikTok’s side. The “Popular” sort is an approximate ordering, not a live view count. The PLN amount is only an estimate: creator diamonds × editable USD/diamond rate × USD/PLN rate; it is not an official TikTok payout. Original artwork comes from TikTok’s public CDN; GiftStream stores their addresses.
